The goal of trying podcasting is to train spoken English and to improve speaking habits. I normally don't talk much, it's difficult to express my own thoughts when I need to speak. I often pause and think about what I'm going to say. So I decided to write something weekly to improve my logical thinking, then translate it into English and record an audio version to train my spoken English.

The content of the podcast may be complex and diverse, not a fixed theme. I've written almost nothing since I started working, podcast content may be boring for a while, I will work on improving this. Normally, I play guitar, read, and go for walks, sometimes get together with friends. If I have a long holiday, I will start a short trip. Most of the time, life is plain, So I hope that making podcasts will push me to discover a different kind of life. Talking to myself  is normal in this podcast, if I practice my spoken English to a certain level, maybe I'll work up the courage to talk with other people.

The podcast is primarily text-based, I record the audio and add a cover image to create a video to post on social media. This channel will not pursue data metrics like views, just for personal documentation. The length of the video is entirely determined by the text, but I hope to speak for 5 to 10 minutes weekly, if the content is very lacking, the video may be short too. If I have the chance, I want to write some short stories, they could also become podcast content.

About the cover. At first, I thought about giving the content to an LLM to generate a cover. After some consideration, I decided just to use some photos I casually took. This project has LLM support, they will help me to deploy the website for the text content, and fix grammar errors in the translation. Although many people say to talk with others you don't need to focus too much on grammar, as long as you can express yourself, I still want my spoken English to be as accurate as possible.

The motivation and goal are quite simple. More importantly, sticking with it is what matters. If I can produce one piece of content weekly, that would be a great change for me. I hope I can achieve it.

See you next week.
